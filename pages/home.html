My Dearest Sister,

There are moments in life when words feel too small to carry the weight of what we feel. Today is one of those moments. As I sit down to write to you, my heart is overflowing with gratitude, love, and a thousand beautiful memories that we've woven together over the years.

You have been my first friend, my safe harbor, my guiding star. Through every storm, you were the lighthouse. Through every celebration, you were the one who made it shine even brighter. You taught me what it means to love without condition, to give without expectation, and to smile even when the world feels heavy.

I remember your laughter — it could fill an entire room with warmth. I remember your hugs — they could heal wounds that no medicine ever could. I remember your patience, your kindness, and the way you always, always believed in me, even when I didn't believe in myself.

On this extraordinary day, I want you to know: you are the most incredible person I have ever known. The world is brighter because you're in it. My life is richer because you're in it. And my heart is fuller because of you.

May this new year of your life bring you all the joy, all the magic, and all the love that you so deeply deserve. You are not just my sister — you are a blessing, a miracle, and the most beautiful soul I know.

With All My Love
